Corporate & Commercial

We provide comprehensive legal counsel to businesses of all sizes, from startups to multinationals. Our approach covers the entire lifecycle of a corporation, ensuring compliance, growth, and risk mitigation.

Company Formation & Structuring

End-to-end guidance on selecting the optimal business entity (LLP, Pvt Ltd, etc.) and jurisdiction to aligned with your commercial goals.

Corporate Governance

Advising boards and management on regulatory compliance, ethical standards, and internal policy frameworks to mitigate liability.

Contract Drafting & Negotiation

Precision drafting of vendor agreements, employment contracts, and service level agreements to protect your commercial interests.

Joint Ventures

Structuring strategic alliances and joint ventures with a focus on clear exit strategies, profit sharing, and operational control.

Regulatory Approvals

Navigating the complex landscape of government licenses, permits, and compliances required for seamless operations.

02

Mergers & Acquisitions

Our M&A practice is renowned for handling complex transactions with speed and precision. We guide clients through every stage of the deal, from due diligence to post-closing integration.

Buy-side Advisory

Strategic target identification, valuation support, and deal structuring to maximize value for acquirers.

Legal Due Diligence

Comprehensive risk assessment covering corporate, tax, employment, and IP liabilities before you commit.

Share Purchase Agreements

Drafting robust transaction documents (SPA, SHA) that keenly define rights, obligations, and indemnity clauses.

Asset Transfers

Facilitating the seamless transfer of business assets, employees, and contracts during restructuring or acquisition.

Cross-border Transactions

Navigating FEMA regulations and international laws to execute seamless cross-border investments and acquisitions.

03

IP & Technology

Protecting your intellectual assets is crucial in today's digital economy. We offer end-to-end IP services, from registration to enforcement and litigation.

Trademark Registration

Strategic brand protection including clearance searches, filing, opposition handling, and portfolio management.

Copyright Protection

Securing creative works including software code, literary content, and artistic creations against infringement.

IP Licensing

Drafting and negotiating licensing and franchising agreements to monetize your intellectual property assets globally.

Data Privacy

Ensuring compliance with the DPDP Act and GDPR, including privacy policy drafting and data breach response.

Tech Transfer

Structuring complex technology transfer agreements for R&D collaborations and commercialization.

04

Tax & Compliance

Navigating India's complex tax landscape requires expert guidance. Our tax practice handles both direct and indirect tax matters, ensuring optimal efficiency and full compliance.

Corporate Tax Structuring

Optimizing tax efficiency for domestic and international operations through strategic entity structuring.

GST Advisory

Expert classification, input tax credit optimization, and compliance management under the GST regime.

International Tax

Advisory on Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA), withholding tax, and cross-border payments.

Tax Litigation

Representing clients before tax tribunals and courts in disputes related to assessments and penalties.

Transfer Pricing

Documentation and advisory services to ensure arm's length pricing in related-party transactions.

Real Estate

We offer extensive legal support for real estate transactions, catering to developers, investors, and individual buyers.

Due Diligence

Thorough title investigation to identify encumbrances, litigation, or regulatory non-compliances before purchase.

Conveyancing

Drafting and registration of Sale Deeds, Lease Deeds, and Agreements to Sell with strict legal safeguards.

Commercial Leasing

Structuring long-term lease agreements for commercial spaces, warehouses, and retail outlets.

RERA Compliance

Land Acquisition

Advising on land aggregation and joint development agreements for large-scale real estate projects.

Project Finance

Structuring and documentation for infrastructure and energy project financing, including consortium lending.

Insolvency & Bankruptcy

Representation for creditors and corporate debtors under the IBC, including resolution plan formulation.

Debt Restructuring

Securitization

Fintech Advisory
